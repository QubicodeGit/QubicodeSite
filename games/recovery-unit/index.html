Recovery Unit

A co-op survival game about a team of scientists travelling by train in search of a cure.

We're still early in development, and the game is a long way from complete. This page is a small look at what we've been working on and where we want to take it.

In the current build

What we can show

The screenshots below show our crew characters and train, forest and desert environments, a research compound, and a relay terminal. These are work-in-progress captures; they don't represent a finished game.

Planned direction

Where we're heading

The idea is for up to four players to use the train as a shared base, head out on missions, and bring back supplies and biological samples to research a cure.

Equipment preparation, recovery objectives, and research aboard the train are part of that plan. There's still a lot to build and test, and the details may change.
