A Routine Check

Wake on a Mars research station, grab your flashlight and spacesuit, and take a terrain scanner out to check on a coworker at the solar panels.

SYS.02

Station Blackout

Rush back as the storm worsens. Search the dark base for a keycard, reach the service room, and restore emergency power to unlock your way out.

SYS.03

Robot Pursuit

Evade a tall humanoid robot inside the station. Restoring power brings red emergency lights and sends it into a frenzy, stronger and faster than before.

SYS.04

Terrain Scanner

Outside, night and a heavy sandstorm leave you almost blind. Use the scanner to locate markers that guide you toward the radio relay.

The scanner's secondary function releases an electromagnetic pulse to stun the robot outside. Use that opening to keep moving.
