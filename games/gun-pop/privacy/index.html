Gun Pop

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the offline Android version of Gun Pop, package com.qubicode.gunpop, version 0.1.0 (2). This version has no advertising, analytics, tracking or real-money purchases.

Who provides the game

Gun Pop is provided by Leo Žigman, trading as Qubicode Games, Croatia. For support or a privacy request, contact qubicode.team@gmail.com.

Information inside the game

This version does not collect or transmit personal information to us. It does not create player accounts, offer chat, request location, access your contacts, camera or microphone, or use advertising identifiers. It does not include active advertising, analytics, attribution or billing services. You do not need an internet connection to play.

Local progress and settings

Progress, coins, unlocked items and settings are stored locally in the app's storage on your device. We cannot access or recover these saves. This version disables Android app backup. To remove the game's local information, use Android Settings → Apps → Gun Pop → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. Removing the data also removes local progress.

Children

Gun Pop is intended for a mixed audience that includes children, with ages 6–8 the youngest intended age group. This offline version does not ask children for personal information. A parent or guardian should contact us for support and avoid sending a child's personal details. If a child sends personal information inadvertently, contact us so we can address the request and delete information that is not necessary to keep.

If you contact support

If you choose to email us outside the app, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you provide. We use them to answer the request, resolve problems and meet applicable legal obligations. Our email provider processes that correspondence. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell personal information.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in answering a parent or user's support enquiry, steps requested before a contract where relevant, or a legal obligation. Keep messages limited to what is needed; please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

Retention, security and your rights

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall. We keep voluntary support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request, manage related follow-up or meet a legal requirement. We limit access to those who need it and delete information when it is no longer needed.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing of information we hold. Contact the email above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Croatia this is AZOP. Requests about records held independently by Google should be directed to Google.

Google Play and this website

Google Play and your device may process installation, diagnostic or store information under Google's own policies. The Play Store delivers this game's asset packs during installation. Google's Play Asset Delivery documentation describes processing device metadata and the application version to deliver the right assets and preserve them after updates; Google says this processing is encrypted, not shared with third parties and subject to a fixed retention period. The game does not use this information for advertising or send player information to us. Visiting this policy in a web browser also involves the website host: GitHub Pages records visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection, GitHub's Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards and policies.

Coins and random rewards

Coins and crates in this version are earned through gameplay. They cannot be purchased with money. Coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed. Crates contain random virtual rewards; their odds and duplicate-item handling are explained in the Workshop. No real-world prizes are offered.

Changes

Any future version that adds online services, advertising or purchases will require an updated policy, accurate store disclosures and appropriate safeguards before those features are made available. We will update the date on this page and notify users where required.
