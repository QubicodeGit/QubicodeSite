Privacy policy.

How information is handled when you visit our website, explore our projects, or get in touch with us.

Last updated 16 October 2026

1. About this policy

Qubicode is the indie team responsible for this website. This policy covers browsing the Qubicode website, including the promotional pages for our games and tools. It does not describe data processed inside our games. Each game has a separate privacy policy page.

For questions about this policy or your information, contact us at contact@example.com.

2. Information and how it is used

You can browse this website without creating an account. It has no registration, checkout, newsletter signup, or contact form, and we do not run advertising trackers or visitor analytics.

If you contact us by email or through our community channels, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address or username, message, and attachments. We use that information to respond to your request, provide support, or discuss working together. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and running a secure website, or on taking steps at your request before entering a contract. We may also process information when necessary to meet a legal obligation.

3. Hosting and external fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub account. GitHub handles these records under its own retention and privacy practices. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Driver Dash promotional page loads fonts from Google Fonts. When that page loads, your browser sends Google your IP address and request information, such as the font requested, browser details, and referring page. See Google Fonts’ privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The website’s own code does not set cookies, store personal information in your browser, or use tracking pixels. Navigation, game filters, and image galleries work without saving a personal profile. Your browser may cache website files to load them faster.

5. Other services and sharing

Links to Discord, YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, or other external websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Following a link may share technical information with that service, and anything you submit there is handled under its rules. Our social links are ordinary links rather than embedded social feeds.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Information you send us may be handled by the email or community service you use, and by team members who need it to address your request. We may disclose information if required by law.

External providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices explain their processing locations and any applicable transfer safeguards.

6. Retention and security

We keep correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry, maintain an ongoing working relationship, or meet applicable legal requirements. The relevant provider controls retention of its hosting logs and service records.

We limit access to correspondence to the people who need it. No internet service can guarantee complete security, so please share only what is necessary for your request.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain uses. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

To make a request about information held by Qubicode, email contact@example.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Requests about records held independently by GitHub, Google, or a social platform should be directed to that provider.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the website or its data practices change and revise the date above. Information about data used inside a particular game belongs in that game’s own policy, linked below.
