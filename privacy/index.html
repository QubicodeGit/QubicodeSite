Qubicode / Privacy

Website privacy policy

This policy covers the Qubicode website and its game information pages. Privacy information for the games themselves is listed separately below.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Information on this website

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, sign-up forms, advertising, or analytics tools. Our website code does not set cookies or store personal information in your browser.

GitHub Pages hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub handles this information under its own Privacy Statement. See GitHub Pages data collection for details.

Fonts and external links

Some game information pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to request those fonts, and Google’s Privacy Policy applies to those requests.

Links to social platforms and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover how those services handle your information.

When you contact us

If you contact Qubicode by email or through a social platform, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and keep it only as long as needed to handle that conversation or meet applicable legal obligations.

For privacy questions, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have shared with us, please use our contact links. The email or social service you use also has its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website or its use of information changes. The date above shows when this policy was last updated.
